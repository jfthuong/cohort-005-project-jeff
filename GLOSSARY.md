# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Admin**:
A member of the Cadence team. Only an Admin decides Refund Requests: they approve or decline them. When this glossary says "the team", it means the Admins.
_Avoid_: Staff, Moderator

**Instructor**:
The person who owns a course. An Instructor doesn't decide Refund Requests on their own courses, because they lose revenue on every approval.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it. The decision is final and is the Admin's judgement: the refund rules are checked when the learner raises the request, not again when it's decided. An Admin can't decide their own Refund Request. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has turned down, with a note telling the learner why. It has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Denied

**Decision**:
An Admin approving or declining an Open Refund Request. The learner is told about every Decision.
_Avoid_: Resolution, Review (for the outcome)

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Payment System**:
The outside service that moves the money. Cadence asks it to send a Refund, and it reports back whether the money was sent. It never decides a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Payment provider, Gateway, Processor

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund that has been handed to the Payment System, which hasn't reported back yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund the Payment System has reported as sent back to the learner. The learner loses access to the course.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund the Payment System has reported it couldn't send. The Refund Request stays approved. The learner keeps access to the course. An Admin can retry it.

**Payout Attempt**:
A single time Cadence hands a Refund to the Payment System, together with what the Payment System reported back. A Refund that has been retried has several Payout Attempts.
_Avoid_: Transaction, Try
